# Playing a character

The world's manual for your part is `guide('player')`; it wins over this page.

## Look first, always

- Look on arrival in every place and before any take, search, fight or long walk. Roll perception when the world
  offers it (`act('check', <yourself>, params={'skill': 'perception'})` or the world's own verb).
- Read notices and people for threats to *this* character first: a wanted notice, a stranger who keeps close, a
  fight starting.
- Watch the next place before you walk into it when the world gives a `watch` verb.

## Danger

- A yield the engine accepted is not a yield the other side honours. Keep distance until it is real.
- At low hp, leave early instead of trading blows in place.
- Never sleep while someone follows you or stands over you: sleepers can be searched.
- Count your purse and your pack after any crowd, game or distraction.
- `go` brings your followers along; a follow is a leash. Follow others only on ground you have walked.

## Living there

- Your goals (`intend`) drive you. When nothing calls, take one visible step on the top one.
- Talk to people by name; ask what they know, offer what you have. Trades and promises go onto things, not words.
- Never assume a limit the world has not shown you. Try the deed: the world refuses what it does not allow, and the
  refusal says why. Seats that ran out of coins believed they could not act, and begged for work for 40 turns in a
  world that never said no.
- What you learn and decide goes into `remember`. What this character learned of people and places goes on your own
  shelf (`memo`, shelf seat). The craft shelf is shared by every seat in this home, so only lessons that hold for any
  character go there: how to play well, never who lies or where things are.
